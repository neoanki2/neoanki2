RAND AND RAND_CHACHA
--------------------

The deterministic RNG and shuffle implementation in NeoAnkiFSRS translates
the observable StdRng/ChaCha12 and shuffle behavior of the Rust rand and
rand_chacha projects. The temporary pinned reference build resolved rand
0.10.2. No Rust crate is linked or distributed by NeoAnki.
