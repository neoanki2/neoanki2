NeoAnki2 Companion

Select text. Add an answer. Keep it.

Connect to NeoAnki2

Keep NeoAnki2 running on this Mac. In its Settings → Local API, turn on Enable local automation API.

Use the port shown in NeoAnki2. The default is 8766.

Dictionary suggestions

Suggestions come from your local NeoAnki2 vocabulary packs. You can always edit the answer.

Add your first card

Pin this extension in Chrome. Highlight a word or phrase, then click its toolbar icon. Review the answer, choose a deck, and save.

Selectable YouTube transcript text works too. Captions playing over a video are not captured automatically.

Selected text and source links stay on this Mac and are sent only to NeoAnki2 when you save. Dictionary lookups also use its local API. Cards follow your NeoAnki2 sync settings.
