Evidence limits

Swift declarations and reference occurrences come from SwiftSyntax. Module dependencies come from SwiftPM and Xcode metadata. The atlas includes both sides of conditional compilation. Named references and possible callers are syntax matches, not compiler-resolved calls or proof of runtime reachability. Unused names are not evidence of dead code.
