# Privacy policy

Effective October 3, 2026. NeoAnki2 is provided by Oleksii Grachov.

## Your study data

NeoAnki2 stores your study library, review history, saved responses, and media
on your device. You can use the app offline without a NeoAnki2 account.
The app does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and does not
send your study data to servers operated by the developer.

## Optional iCloud sync

iCloud sync is off until you enable it. Before its first upload, the app creates
a local backup. When enabled, library content and associated media synchronize
through your private CloudKit database in your Apple iCloud account. The
developer does not have access to that private database. Apple provides this
service under its [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Disabling sync stops future synchronization on that device. It does not delete
copies already stored in iCloud or on your other devices. Manage those copies
through your Apple account and device settings. Export a backup before deleting
any app data.

## Camera, microphone, photos, and notifications

Camera, microphone, and photo access let you add media to study items or record
answers. Media remains in your library and is included in private iCloud sync
when you enable it. The app requests notification access only when you enable
study reminders. Due Cards widgets use shared on-device storage to show
aggregate due information. You can revoke permissions in device Settings.

## Imports, exports, and support

Files you import become part of your local library. Exported files can contain
study content and media; you decide where to save or share them. Links to the
documentation and support website open external web pages, whose hosts may
process ordinary web requests under their own policies.

If you contact support by email or through GitHub, the information you choose
to send is used to respond to your request. Do not attach private libraries or
media to public issues. See [support](../support/) for reporting guidance.

## Retention and changes

Your study data remains on your devices and, if enabled, in your private iCloud
database until you remove it. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily remove
iCloud copies, exported files, or backups. This policy will be updated if the
app's data practices change.
