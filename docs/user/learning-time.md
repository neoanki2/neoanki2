# Learning time

Each deck home shows **Today** and **All time** learning-time totals. Choose
**View Learning Time** for Today, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, or All Time and an
expandable deck breakdown. Session completion also shows the session's duration.
Tracking starts with your first study session after installing this feature;
past review durations are not imported.

## What counts

The timer starts when a study card is available and the study surface is active.
Reading the prompt or answer, answering, comparing responses, recording, and
playing learning audio or video count. Skipped cards and unfinished attempts
keep their time. Undoing a grade or resetting progress does not remove time.
Browsing, editing, loading, saving, completion screens, background time, and
hidden study surfaces do not count.

After two minutes without an interaction, the timer pauses. Those first two
minutes count; later idle time does not. Interacting with study resumes it.
Audio/video playback and recording keep the timer active, but animated images
do not. Use **Pause Timer** for a deliberate break and **Resume Timer** to return.
A manual pause stays paused until you resume it.

Time is saved every 15 seconds and when a card or session ends, the timer pauses,
or the app moves to the background. An unexpected shutdown can lose time since
the latest saved checkpoint. A save error appears beside the timer and remains
visible on completion and totals screens after you leave study. Unsaved segments
are retained in memory and retried while the app remains open; **Retry Saving**
also starts another save attempt.

## Deck totals

Time follows the item's **current** deck. Moving an item moves all its previous
time; moving a subdeck changes its parents' totals. **This deck only** excludes
children, while a parent's headline total includes all its subdecks. Expanded
child rows explain the parent total; they are not additional time.

Items without a deck appear under **Unassigned**. Deleting an item preserves its
time under **Deleted Content**, included in All Decks but outside existing deck
totals. Changing a card setup or regenerating cards for a surviving item does not
erase that item's time.

Today and the rolling seven- and thirty-day periods follow your configured
study-day rollover (4 a.m. by default) in the viewing device's time zone.
Sessions crossing a boundary contribute only their time inside the period.

## Across devices

With iCloud sync enabled, updated Mac, iPhone, and iPad apps combine learning time,
including offline study after it uploads. Distinct simultaneous sessions on two
devices both contribute; repeated synchronization never duplicates a segment.

Activity uses a separate private iCloud zone. Older apps continue ordinary
library sync, but must be updated to record or display learning time. Temporarily
missing synced items wait for their content or deletion record before appearing
in a deck total; the library report labels that time as waiting for synced content. Imported decks and local API review submissions do not import
or manufacture learning time.
